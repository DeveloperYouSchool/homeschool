# Hans's favorite topic  

Hans's favorite topic is phishing. Let's let that sit for a minute.  

But Hans is also very concerned that your homeschool student may actually be the fish.  

Look at your student's email with them. Many email systems have filters to block these type of messages, so maybe check the 'junk' or 'spam' folders.  

Phishing is a type of criminal activity to get your student to either give away personal information or give access to their phone, tablet, or computer.  

Just like the physical activity of fishing in a body of water, criminals use techniques to get the fish to 'bite'.  

What are these tools?  

Environment - criminals know where their victim's attention is focused.
Lure - criminals want an emotional response in their victim.  
Hook - criminals need that emotional response to trigger an immediate action.  

Think of the fish in the lake. Lures often have something that attracts attention and looks just enough like food to get the fish to act.  

If a peanut butter sandwich was dropped in the lake, it's unlikely a fish would bite it
